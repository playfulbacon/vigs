# Interview leads

Things heard informally that need confirming in interviews before they go into the dataset. Nothing here is verified, and nothing here is shown on the site. Don't record addresses; describe houses by neighbourhood at most, and only once the people who lived there agree.

When a lead is confirmed, turn it into records: a `data/places/` entry for the house (`kind: house`, no address) and `memberships` on each resident (`place`, `role: resident`, `start`/`end`), cited to the interview claims. That's how Indie House is modelled.

## The hand-me-down houses

A recurring pattern worth asking about directly: shared houses that passed from one group of developers to the next, and kept being where the scene gathered. Indie House (Richmond, 2012–2016) is the documented example.

### Indie House (Richmond, 2012–2016)

The documented house. Leads that add to it:

- *Lead noted November 2026.* **The Northways are still growing plants that came from Indie House.** They aren't documented as residents, so the plants were passed on to them, which makes this a lovely object to anchor a story.
  - To ask: which plants, who grew them at Indie House, when and how they reached the Northways (when the house ended in late 2016?), and whether anyone else kept something from the house.
  - A possible photo for the project, with permission.
  - Ask: Sarah and Colin Northway; the Indie House residents.

### Kramer's house (party house)

*Lead noted November 2026.*

- Someone known as **Kramer** held parties at their house, before the Northways became the scene's party hosts.
- **Greg Lobanov** later moved into Kramer's house and held parties there.
- Then **daffodil** moved in and held parties there.

To confirm:
- Who is Kramer (full name, or a nickname)? Who is "daffodil"?
- Roughly where (neighbourhood only) and when: when did Kramer host, when did Greg move in (after Indie House ended in late 2016?), and when did daffodil take over?
- What were the parties like, who came, and how did they fit alongside Full Indie meetups?
- When and why did the Northways become the hosts?

Ask: Greg Lobanov, Sarah and Colin Northway, Kramer and daffodil once identified.

*Lead noted November 2026.*

- **Dylan** was part of it. This is probably **Dylan Gedig** (Red Nexus Games, Peglin; University of Victoria roots), but that's unconfirmed.

To confirm:

Ask: Dylan Gedig, the Northways (University of Victoria), IGDA Victoria organizers.
