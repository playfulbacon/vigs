# Vancouver Indie Studios, Audio Houses, and Games Missing from the Draft Timeline

Scope note: these notes add to the draft at `27587a9d-vancouver-indie-timeline.md`. The research budget covered roughly 25 fetches and searches, so some requested items (Steam URLs, Captain Games, Happy Ray, Hothead, The Coalition, Kabam) are still gaps, listed at the end of each section. Wikipedia infobox data was read through WebFetch summaries. Where Wikipedia carries a maintenance flag, it is noted.

## 1. Audio studios: A Shell in the Pit and Power Up Audio

### Takeaway
Vancouver has two of indie gaming's best-known audio houses:
- **Power Up Audio** was founded by Kevin Regamey and Jeff Tangsoc and was active by 2012. It broke through on TowerFall at Indie House, then did Crypt of the NecroDancer, Darkest Dungeon (on a royalty deal) and Celeste (GDC Best Audio 2019).
- **A Shell in the Pit** grew out of Gordon McGladdery's music work on Rogue Legacy. The studio dates itself to 2014. It has since shipped more than 70 titles, including Night in the Woods, Untitled Goose Game and Chicory.

Correction to the brief: A Shell in the Pit was **not** founded by Em Halberstadt. Halberstadt is Creative Director.

### Cited Findings

**A Shell in the Pit (ASITP)**
- **Location:** Vancouver, BC. — [ashellinthepit.com](https://ashellinthepit.com/)
- **Founding:**
  - The studio's press page gives 2014 as the founding year. It says the studio "grew out of" Gordon McGladdery's music, and that he started in games doing sound and music for *Rogue Legacy*. When sound design work piled up, he hired his first employees: Em, Joey and Rachel. — [ASITP Press](https://ashellinthepit.com/press)
  - The Georgia Straight (2024) says the company began making sound for games in **2012**, which conflicts with the 2014 date. One way to reconcile them: McGladdery's solo work (Rogue Legacy shipped in 2013) came before the incorporated studio. — [Georgia Straight 2024](https://www.straight.com/tech/how-vancouver-became-a-hub-for-indie-video-games)
- **Leadership on the site:** Gordon McGladdery (Founder/Composer), Rachel Sim (Studio Director), Em Halberstadt (Creative Director), Bryson Dodwell (CFO/CTO), Jason McKinlay (Trailers), plus about a dozen sound designers and programmers. — [ashellinthepit.com](https://ashellinthepit.com/)
  - The Georgia Straight calls McGladdery "studio director." The current site gives that title to Rachel Sim, so the role has probably changed hands. — [Georgia Straight 2024](https://www.straight.com/tech/how-vancouver-became-a-hub-for-indie-video-games)
- **Size:** Ten people in 2021 and 16 full-time staff now. The studio moved to a four-day flexible work week in 2022. — [ASITP Press](https://ashellinthepit.com/press)
- **Credits:** More than 70 shipped titles.
  - Highlighted on the press page: *Untitled Goose Game*, *Pacific Drive*, *Chicory: A Colorful Tale*, *FNAF: Security Breach*, *Caves of Qud*, *Thank Goodness You're Here!*, *Rogue Legacy 1 & 2* and *Night in the Woods*. — [ASITP Press](https://ashellinthepit.com/press)
  - The site's team profiles also list *Parkitect*, *Wandersong*, *Ikenfell*, *Beastieball*, *Tchia*, *Cozy Grove*, *Godfall*, *Full Metal Furies*, *Poly Bridge 2*, *Monaco 2*, *Kingdom Two Crowns: Deadlands*, *Little Kitty Big City*, *Potionomics*, *JETT: The Far Shore*, *Fantastic Contraption*, *Brigador* and *Mineko's Night Market*.
  - Trailer audio for *Manifold Garden*, *Crypt of the NecroDancer*, *Before Your Eyes* and *Cobalt Core*. — [ashellinthepit.com](https://ashellinthepit.com/)
- **Credits on Vancouver games:**
  - Wandersong, Chicory and Beastieball (Greg Lobanov)
  - Ikenfell (Chevy Ray)
  - Fantastic Contraption (Northways)
  - Night in the Woods (Indie House era)
  - Source: [ashellinthepit.com](https://ashellinthepit.com/)
- **Own game:** *Fish Game* (2023), an aquarium simulator. — [ASITP Press](https://ashellinthepit.com/press) · [Georgia Straight 2024](https://www.straight.com/tech/how-vancouver-became-a-hub-for-indie-video-games)
- **Awards on games it worked on, listed by the studio:**
  - IGF Seumas McNally Grand Prize and D.I.C.E. Game of the Year 2020 (*Untitled Goose Game*)
  - GDCA Game of the Year (*Untitled Goose Game*); the press page says 2019, but the GDCAs for 2019 games were held in 2020
  - BAFTA Family 2020 (*Untitled Goose Game*) and 2021 (*Chicory*)
  - The Game Awards Games for Impact 2023 (*Tchia*)
  - BAFTA British Game 2025 (*Thank Goodness You're Here!*)
  - Source: [ASITP Press](https://ashellinthepit.com/press)
- **Panel:** AirCon Vault 2023 hosted a panel with Em Halberstadt, Rachel Sim and Jamie Lee. — [Airwiggles](https://www.airwiggles.com/c/conference/aircon-vault-2023-panel-a-shell-in-the-pit-em-halberstadt-rachel-sim-jamie-lee)
- **Podcast:** XJ Music's podcast episode 5 interviews McGladdery. Not opened. — [XJ Music podcast](https://xjmusic.com/podcast/ep5/)

**Power Up Audio (PUA)**
- **Founders:** Jeff Tangsoc and Kevin Regamey. They met at a large commercial sound design studio, where they were kept out of client contact and handed "a shopping list of sounds." Regamey called it "a lifestyle of distrust." — [EGM: Sonic Boom](https://egmnow.com/sonic-boom-how-vancouvers-power-up-audio-became-an-indie-powerhouse/)
- **Founding year:** Not confirmed by a primary source.
  - EGM mentions a 2012 SFX reel from "early in the company's history." — [EGM](https://egmnow.com/sonic-boom-how-vancouvers-power-up-audio-became-an-indie-powerhouse/)
  - A game-outsourcing directory lists 2013. — [gamedevoutsourcing.com](https://www.gamedevoutsourcing.com/company/power-up-audio)
- **Breaking into games:**
  - They cold-called developers, redesigned game trailers as samples, and volunteered at Summer Games Done Quick and Vancouver game jams.
  - Regamey got to know Noel Berry and Chevy Ray Johnston at the 2012 Full Indie Game Jam. Matt (Maddy) Thorson's TowerFall team then invited PUA to Indie House.
  - PUA's TowerFall SFX work started at the 2013 Global Game Jam. TowerFall led to *Crypt of the NecroDancer* and *Darkest Dungeon*.
  - Source: [EGM](https://egmnow.com/sonic-boom-how-vancouvers-power-up-audio-became-an-indie-powerhouse/)
- **Darkest Dungeon:** PUA negotiated a royalty deal with Red Hook, voiced many enemies, and consulted on narration and playtesting. — [EGM](https://egmnow.com/sonic-boom-how-vancouvers-power-up-audio-became-an-indie-powerhouse/)
- **Awards:**
  - *Celeste*: PUA did all sound design and some music support alongside composer Lena Raine. Regamey's work won Best Audio at the 2019 Game Developers Choice Awards.
  - *Into the Breach*: Tangsoc joined Matthew Davis on stage at the 2019 BAFTA Games Awards.
  - Source: [EGM](https://egmnow.com/sonic-boom-how-vancouvers-power-up-audio-became-an-indie-powerhouse/)
- **Credits on the official site:**
  - *Subnautica 2*, *DELTARUNE Chapter 5*, *Cult of the Lamb: Woolhaven*, *Witchbrook*, *TUNIC*, *Rift of the NecroDancer*
  - *Darkest Dungeon* and *Darkest Dungeon 2*, *Ruined King*, *Cadence of Hyrule*, *Celeste*, *Into the Breach*
  - *Industries of Titan*, *Phantom Brigade*, *Subnautica: Below Zero*, *Darksiders Genesis*, *Super Meat Boy Forever*
  - *Descenders*, *TowerFall Ascension*, *Mushroom 11*, *Timespinner*, *Tooth and Tail*, *Griftlands* and others
  - Source: [powerupaudio.com](https://powerupaudio.com/)
- **Size:** Five people at the time of the EGM piece: Tangsoc, Regamey, Joey Godard, Craig Barnes and Cole Verderber. They work from home offices in Vancouver and have turned down projects that would have pushed them to as many as 14 people. — [EGM](https://egmnow.com/sonic-boom-how-vancouvers-power-up-audio-became-an-indie-powerhouse/)
- **Celeste audio release:** PUA released *Celeste*'s FMOD project and source audio publicly for educational and modding use. — [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/audio/-i-celeste-s-i-audio-files-are-now-publicly-available-for-devs) · [PUA on X](https://twitter.com/powerupaudio/status/1235086609302712320)
- **Regamey alias:** A Celeste wiki entry identifies Regamey as "otdq." Fan source. — [Celeste Wiki](https://celeste.ink/wiki/Kevin_Regamey)

### Inferences
- The Vancouver indie hits (TowerFall, NecroDancer, Darkest Dungeon, Celeste, Wandersong, Chicory, Ikenfell, Beastieball) share a small set of local audio vendors. These two studios link the games to one another and are good oral-history subjects.
- PUA's origin story runs through Full Indie Game Jam 2012 to Indie House to TowerFall. That fits the draft timeline's 2012–2013 entries directly.
- Brace Yourself and Klei titles (Phantom Brigade, Industries of Titan, Rift of the NecroDancer, Griftlands) appear in PUA's credits, so local studios hire local audio.

### Gaps
- PUA's exact founding year (2012 or 2013) is unconfirmed by a primary source.
- ASITP's exact role per game (music or SFX) is not broken down on the site.
- Night in the Woods' Wikipedia page credits the music to Holowka and Benson. ASITP's role there is presumably sound design, but this is unverified.
- Other Vancouver game-audio people were not researched: Lena Raine is associated with Vancouver games, but her residence is unconfirmed. Jonah Senzel (Inscryption composer) was not checked either.

## 2. Klei Entertainment: game list, Tencent deal, publishing

### Takeaway
Jamie Cheng founded Klei in Vancouver in July 2005, with Jeffrey Agala as co-founder and creative director. It has shipped about 15 games, from *Eets* (2006) to *Rotwood* (2026). Tencent took a majority stake in January 2021, with Klei keeping creative control. Klei also publishes other studios' games, including Vancouver's NecroDancer games and Beastieball.

### Cited Findings
- **Founding and Tencent:** Founded July 2005 by Jamie Cheng in Vancouver; Jeffrey Agala is co-founder and creative director. In January 2021 Tencent acquired a majority stake. No percentage was disclosed, and Klei "would remain in full control of its products." — [Wikipedia: Klei Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klei_Entertainment)
- **Games developed:**
  - 2006: *Eets*
  - 2008: *N+* (engine work)
  - 2010: *Shank* (*Sugar Rush* was cancelled)
  - 2012: *Shank 2*, *Mark of the Ninja*
  - 2013: *Don't Starve*
  - 2014: *Eets Munchies*
  - 2015: *Invisible, Inc.*
  - 2016: *Don't Starve Together*
  - 2018: *Mark of the Ninja: Remastered*
  - 2019: *Oxygen Not Included*
  - *Hot Lava* (no year given in the article)
  - 2021: *Griftlands*
  - 2026: *Rotwood*
  - *Dread Pilots*: early access 2024
  - *Away Team*: TBA
  - *Don't Starve Elsewhere*: announced April 9, 2026
  - Source: [Wikipedia: Klei Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klei_Entertainment)
- **Games published:**
  - *Crypt of the NecroDancer* (2015) and *Rift of the NecroDancer* (2025), both by Brace Yourself Games
  - *Mind Over Magic* (Sparkypants), early access 2023
  - *Lab Rat* (Chump Squad), TBA
  - *Beastieball* (Wishes Unlimited), early access 2024
  - Source: [Wikipedia: Klei Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klei_Entertainment)
- **Beastieball:**
  - Early access on Windows on November 12, 2024, published by Klei. Platforms are macOS and Windows.
  - The 1.0 release is "expected" in 2027.
  - Team: Greg Lobanov (director), Alexis Dean-Jones (art), Lena Raine (music).
  - Source: [Wikipedia: Beastieball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beastieball)
  - The Georgia Straight called Wishes Unlimited a four-person studio in 2024. — [Georgia Straight 2024](https://www.straight.com/tech/how-vancouver-became-a-hub-for-indie-video-games)

### Inferences
- Klei publishing *Crypt of the NecroDancer* in 2015 is an early case of a Vancouver indie publishing a Vancouver indie. Beastieball (2024) is a later one. Together they could be a timeline thread: "Klei as scene anchor."

### Gaps
- Exact release dates (month and day) for each Klei title were not gathered.
- The Tencent stake percentage was not disclosed.
- Rotwood's 1.0 date in 2026 and Steam URLs were not verified.

## 3. Draft studios: confirming Vancouver location and game lists

### Takeaway
Confirmed as Vancouver-based: Brace Yourself Games, Extremely OK Games, Daniel Mullins Games, Northway Games, Wishes Unlimited and Red Hook Studios. Red Hook (*Darkest Dungeon*) is **missing from the draft** and should be added. Red Nexus Games (*Peglin*) is BC-based with Victoria roots. *A Short Hike* (Toronto) and Ivy Road (Austin) should be excluded.

### Cited Findings

**Brace Yourself Games**
- Founded 2013 by Ryan Clark in Vancouver, with about 20 employees as of 2025. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brace_Yourself_Games). The article carries an "LLM-generated text" maintenance flag, so treat it with care.
- **Games:**
  - *Crypt of the NecroDancer* (2015)
  - *Cadence of Hyrule* (2019; published by Nintendo, Switch)
  - *Phantom Brigade* (2020; made after BYG acquired developer Tetragon Works in 2018)
  - *Industries of Titan* (2023)
  - *Rift of the NecroDancer* (February 2025; Windows and Switch)
- **Publishing:** Launched the Brace Yourself Publishing label in 2022. Its titles include *Cobalt Core* (Rocket Rat Games, 2023) and *Super Raft Boat Together* (2023).
- **Layoffs:** About half the staff was laid off in May 2023.
- Source for all of the above: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brace_Yourself_Games)

**Extremely OK Games (EXOK) / Matt Makes Games**
- **History:** Matt Makes Games was incorporated in November 2012 and later renamed Maddy Makes Games. The team became Extremely OK Games, announced September 5, 2019 and technically founded March 2019. Offices are in Vancouver. — [Wikipedia (Maddy Thorson / EXOK)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extremely_OK_Games)
- **Games:**
  - *TowerFall* (2013, Ouya exclusive) and *TowerFall Ascension* (2014; more than US$500k in its first month)
  - *Celeste Classic* (PICO-8, 2015; made in a four-day jam with Noel Berry, August 2015)
  - *Celeste* (2018; more than 500k copies by the end of 2018)
  - *Celeste Classic 2: Lani's Trek* (2021)
  - *Celeste 64: Fragments of the Mountain* (2024)
  - Romhacks *Sure Shot* (2022) and *Masterpiece* (2025)
  - *Earthblade*: cancelled January 2025
  - Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extremely_OK_Games)
- **Recognition:** Thorson was on the Forbes 30 Under 30 list in 2014. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extremely_OK_Games)

**Daniel Mullins Games**
- Headquartered in Vancouver. Mullins worked as a programmer at Vancouver studios before going indie. His games are *Pony Island*, *The Hex* and *Inscryption*. — search summary citing [Gematsu company page](https://www.gematsu.com/companies/daniel-mullins-games) and [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/how-game-jam-sacrifices-became-inscryption)
- ***Inscryption*:**
  - Published by Devolver Digital, Unity engine, music by Jonah Senzel.
  - Grew out of Mullins's Ludum Dare 43 (2018) entry, *Sacrifices Must Be Made*.
  - Release dates: Windows October 19, 2021; Linux and Mac June 23, 2022; PS4/PS5 August 30, 2022; Switch December 1, 2022; Xbox April 10, 2023.
  - More than 1 million copies sold by January 2022.
  - Awards: GDCA Game of the Year (22nd); IGF 2022 Seumas McNally Grand Prize plus Excellence in Design, Narrative and Audio (the first game to win both top prizes); BAFTA Game Design; Polygon's best game of 2021.
  - Source: [Wikipedia: Inscryption](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inscryption)

**Northway Games (Sarah and Colin Northway)**
- Based in Vancouver. Their games include *Fantastic Contraption* (2008), the *Rebuild* series (Sarah) and *Incredipede* (2012). — [Georgia Straight 2024](https://www.straight.com/tech/how-vancouver-became-a-hub-for-indie-video-games)
- ***I Was a Teenage Exocolonist*:**
  - Released August 25, 2022, published by Finji.
  - Platforms: Windows, macOS, Linux, Switch, PS4 and PS5. The infobox also lists Switch 2.
  - Won the Canadian Screen Award for Best Video Game and the Games for Change Best Gameplay award. IGF Honorable Mention for Visual Arts.
  - Nominated at The Game Awards 2022 (Games for Impact), D.I.C.E., GDCA, BAFTA and GLAAD.
  - Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Was_a_Teenage_Exocolonist)
- **2026 status:** Still active and supporting Exocolonist. Build 6602 added Steam Workshop modding for the fourth "Exoversary" in August 2026, and plushie campaigns ran in 2024–2026. No new game was announced on the site. — [northwaygames.com](https://northwaygames.com/)

**Greg Lobanov / Happy Ray / Finji / Wishes Unlimited**
- ***Chicory: A Colorful Tale*:**
  - By Greg Lobanov, published by Finji.
  - Released June 10, 2021 on Windows, macOS, PS4 and PS5; Switch in December 2021; Xbox in May 2023.
  - Started as *Drawdog* and went to Kickstarter in August 2019. Music by Lena Raine, lead art by Alexis Dean-Jones.
  - Won a BAFTA Family award and the Pégases Best Foreign Independent award; 96% of critics recommend it on OpenCritic.
  - Source: [Wikipedia: Chicory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicory:_A_Colorful_Tale)
- Lobanov's next studio is Wishes Unlimited (four people in 2024), making *Beastieball*. — [Georgia Straight 2024](https://www.straight.com/tech/how-vancouver-became-a-hub-for-indie-video-games)
- Finji itself is not a Vancouver company. It publishes Vancouver games: Night in the Woods, Chicory and Exocolonist. Finji's location was not verified here.

**Red Hook Studios (missing from the draft)**
- Founded by Chris Bourassa and Tyler Sigman in BC. The studio formed around April 2013. — [Wikipedia: Darkest Dungeon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darkest_Dungeon)
- The Georgia Straight calls Red Hook the "Vancouver team behind Darkest Dungeon," founded by former triple-A developers. — [Georgia Straight 2024](https://www.straight.com/tech/how-vancouver-became-a-hub-for-indie-video-games)
- ***Darkest Dungeon*:**
  - Steam early access February 3, 2015; full release on Windows and OS X January 19, 2016.
  - Ports: Linux April 2016; PS4/Vita September 2016; iPad August 2017; Switch January 2018; Xbox One February 2018.
  - Sales: more than 650k in its first week, 1M by November 2016, 2M by December 2017.
  - Three IGF 2016 nominations: Grand Prize, Visual Art and Audio. Narrated by Wayne June.
  - Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darkest_Dungeon)
- ***Darkest Dungeon II*:** Announced February 2019. Released on Windows in May 2023 after about 18 months in early access. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darkest_Dungeon)

**Night in the Woods (for the 2017 draft entry)**
- Developed by Infinite Fall with Secret Lab and published by Finji.
- Team: Alec Holowka, Scott Benson and Bethany Hockenberry.
- Release dates: PC and PS4 February 21, 2017; Xbox One December 2017; Switch February 2018; iOS September 2021.
- Won the IGF Seumas McNally Grand Prize and a BAFTA for Narrative.
- Kickstarter: the US$50k goal was met in 26 hours, and the campaign raised more than $200k.
- Its Wikipedia page does not mention Vancouver or Indie House.
- Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Night_in_the_Woods)

**Red Nexus Games / *Peglin* (BC, likely Victoria)**
- **Studio:** Founded 2015 (some sources say 2016) by Dylan Gedig. Described as BC-based, with roots at the University of Victoria. A four-person team. — search summaries of [The GDWC](https://thegdwc.com/teams/rng) · [Shacknews](https://shacknews.com/article/127914/peglin-developer-talks-making-the-game-with-a-four-person-team)
- ***Peglin*:**
  - Born at Orca Jam 2019. Won an Indie Game Award for Best Design. — [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/press-release/from-game-jam-to-winning-best-design-the-story-behind-peglin)
  - Steam early access April 25, 2022; mobile early access April 11, 2023; 1.0 and Switch August 27, 2024.
  - Published by IndieArk, BlitWorks and Red Nexus.
  - About 400k early access copies sold in the first year.
  - Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peglin)

**Excluded after checking**
- *A Short Hike*: Adam Robinson-Yu is "Toronto-based." — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Short_Hike)
- Ivy Road (*Wanderstop*, 2025, Annapurna): Wikipedia places it in Austin, Texas, and it shut down March 31, 2026. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wanderstop)
  - However, the Georgia Straight 2024 article includes Davey Wreden and Karla Zimonja in its Vancouver story and calls Ivy Road an "international studio." — [Georgia Straight 2024](https://www.straight.com/tech/how-vancouver-became-a-hub-for-indie-video-games)
  - This is a conflict. Wreden may be Vancouver-resident. Verify in an interview before listing.

### Inferences
- The draft's 2015–2016 period should add Red Hook's *Darkest Dungeon*: early access February 2015, release January 2016, 2M copies by 2017. It is one of the scene's biggest commercial hits and is tied to Power Up Audio.
- Peglin belongs in a "Vancouver Island / Victoria" sidebar, alongside Hinterland (Section 4).

### Gaps
- Captain Games / Blinkbat (Justin Smith) were not researched beyond the draft.
- Happy Ray Games (Lobanov's earlier label) was not separately verified.
- Wishes Unlimited's founding year is unknown.
- Red Hook's 2025–2026 status (DLC, layoffs) is unknown, and its own Wikipedia page returned 404.
- Steam store URLs for box art were not collected for any title; the report writer should not invent them.
- Pony Island's release date and The Hex (2018) details were not fetched.

## 4. Other Vancouver / BC independents and mid-size studios

### Takeaway
The main mid-size independents are Phoenix Labs (2014; *Dauntless*, *Fae Farm*; heavily cut by 2025), Blackbird Interactive (2007; Homeworld; still active) and Hinterland (*The Long Dark*). Hinterland is on Vancouver Island (Comox Valley), not in Vancouver. Other names in directories, such as Smoking Gun Interactive, need verifying.

### Cited Findings

**Phoenix Labs** (Vancouver)
- Founded April 2014 by ex-Riot developers Jesse Houston, Sean Bender and Robin Mayne. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoenix_Labs)
- **Games:**
  - *Dauntless*: open beta on PC May 2018; full console release September 26, 2019.
  - *Fae Farm* (2023).
- **Ownership:**
  - Garena acquired the studio in January 2020, reportedly for about US$150M.
  - A management buyout in February 2023.
  - In May 2024 it was reported that blockchain firm Forte had quietly acquired it.
- **Layoffs:**
  - 9% of staff in May 2023, then 34 more in December 2023.
  - More than 100 in May 2024, with projects cancelled.
  - "Most of the remaining development team" let go in January 2025.
  - Wikipedia describes the studio in the past tense.
- Source for all of the above: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoenix_Labs)

**Blackbird Interactive** (Vancouver)
- **Founding:** Founded 2007 by ex-Relic and EA Canada staff, including CEO Rob Cunningham and CCO Jon Aaron Kambeitz, who were founding members of Relic and worked on Homeworld. Note: the brief said 2010; Wikipedia says 2007. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackbird_Interactive)
- **Games:**
  - *Homeworld: Deserts of Kharak* (January 20, 2016, Gearbox)
  - *Minecraft Earth* (2019, assisting Mojang)
  - *Hardspace: Shipbreaker* (early access June 16, 2020; 1.0 2022; published by Focus)
  - *Crossfire: Legion* (2022)
  - *Minecraft Legends* (2023, with Mojang)
  - *Homeworld 3* (May 13, 2024, Gearbox)
  - *Earthless* (early access 2024)
- **Layoffs:** 41 laid off in August 2023, plus another round in February 2024.
- **2025:** In August 2025 the studio bought the Hardspace IP from Focus and signed with publisher Lyrical Games for a new title.
- Source for all of the above: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackbird_Interactive)

**Hinterland Studio** (Vancouver Island, Comox Valley)
- **Studio:** Raphael van Lierop moved from Vancouver to the Comox Valley and formed Hinterland there as a mostly remote team. The team and the Kickstarter were announced in September 2013. — [Wikipedia: The Long Dark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Long_Dark)
- ***The Long Dark*:**
  - Steam early access September 2014; full release August 1, 2017.
  - Ports: Switch September 17, 2020; Xbox Series June 19, 2025; PS5 November 11, 2025.
  - Sales: 3.3M by September 2019 and 5M by 2021.
  - Final Wintermute episode, *The Light at the End of All Things*: March 30, 2026.
  - Sequel *Blackfrost: The Long Dark 2* announced December 12, 2024.
  - Source: [Wikipedia: The Long Dark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Long_Dark)

**Other names from directories (unverified)**
- Directories also list Smoking Gun Interactive (founded by Company of Heroes veterans). — [Built In Vancouver](https://builtinvancouver.org/articles/game-companies-vancouver) · [gamecompanies.com](https://gamecompanies.com/industries/north-america/canadian-game-industry/british-columbia/vancouver) · [gamedevmap](https://www.gamedevmap.com/index.php?location=Vancouver)

### Inferences
- The 2023–2025 layoffs at Phoenix Labs, Blackbird and Brace Yourself suggest Vancouver's mid-size independents were hit by the same downturn the draft notes for 2023. A "contraction" entry would be justified.

### Gaps
- Not researched within the budget: Hothead Games, Kabam Vancouver, The Coalition, Kinematic, Sad Owl and the other speculative names in the brief. None should be included without verification.
- The status of *Dauntless* servers was not covered by the Wikipedia fetch.
- Full Indie Summit speaker lists and IGF finalist lists by Vancouver origin were not mined.
- Steam and official URLs for box art were not collected.
