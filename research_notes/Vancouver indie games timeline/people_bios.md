# People of the Vancouver Indie Game Scene: Bio Cards

Research notes, compiled November 2026. Scope: short, sourced bio cards to seed a "characters" dataset for an oral history. Rules followed: only publicly documented personal details; birth dates only where a source documents them (and the source is named); uncertainty flagged. "Not found" means I searched and found no reliable public source, not that the fact doesn't exist. Photo licenses are **unverified** unless stated: open the Wikimedia Commons file page before reusing any image.

Source-quality note: several Wikipedia pages were read via WebFetch summaries of en.wikipedia.org. The Brace Yourself Games page carries a Wikipedia notice that parts may be AI-generated. Mirror sites (wiki.krisyotam.com, cavac.at, kaweah.freedombox.rocks) are treated as low-confidence.

## 1. AAA roots: Don Mattrick and Jeff Sember (Distinctive Software)

### Takeaway
Mattrick is thoroughly documented: born in Vancouver, co-founded Distinctive Software at 17, later led EA Worldwide Studios, Xbox and Zynga. Sember has almost no public footprint beyond co-founding DSI and selling his stake in 1986.

### Cited Findings
**Don Mattrick**
- Full name Donald Allan Mattrick; born February 13, 1964, in Vancouver (per Wikipedia; I didn't check the underlying citation) — [Wikipedia: Don Mattrick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Mattrick)
- Studied business and managerial economics at Simon Fraser University. Honorary Doctor of Laws from SFU in 1999 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Mattrick)
- Co-founded Distinctive Software Inc. (DSI) with Jeff Sember in Vancouver in 1982, aged 17. Sember sold his stake to Mattrick in 1986. EA acquired DSI in July 1991 (it became EA Canada) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Mattrick)
- About 15 years at EA, including president of Worldwide Studios. Wikipedia gives two departure dates, 2005 and February 2007, and doesn't reconcile them — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Mattrick)
- Joined Microsoft in July 2007 as SVP for Xbox 360 and PC gaming. Credited with Kinect. President of the Interactive Entertainment Business from October 2010. Left July 1, 2013 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Mattrick)
- Zynga CEO from July 2013; resigned April 8, 2015 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Mattrick)
- **Current status:** co-founder and co-president of MdGB Capital (private asset management and tech advisory). Member of the Order of British Columbia (2024). On the Vancouver Prostate Centre board since 2019 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Mattrick)
- **Photo:** Wikipedia infobox file "66-Mattrick.jpg" (caption "Mattrick in 2010"). License not verified — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Mattrick)
- **Timeline links:** 1982 DSI founding; 1991 EA acquisition (EA Canada, Burnaby) — [MobyGames: Distinctive Software](https://www.mobygames.com/company/24/)

**Jeff Sember**
- Co-founded DSI with Mattrick in 1982; sold his stake to Mattrick in 1986 — [Wikipedia: Don Mattrick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Mattrick)

### Inferences
- Mattrick is a "patriarch" figure for the AAA prehistory, not an indie participant. His ties to the indie era are indirect: EA Canada alumni seeded later studios.

### Gaps
- Jeff Sember: no birth year, background, post-1986 career, current status or photo found. A web search for "Jeff Sember Distinctive Software" returned nothing relevant. MobyGames credits or SFU/BC business press archives may help.
- Mattrick's official social links: none found or checked.

## 2. Klei Entertainment: Jamie Cheng, Jeffrey Agala and early leaders

### Takeaway
Jamie Cheng (SFU computer science, then a Relic AI programmer) founded Klei in July 2005, funding it by selling his THQ shares and borrowing $10,000 from his brother. Jeffrey Agala is the documented co-founder and Creative Director. Tencent took majority ownership in January 2021. Klei is a key publisher of Vancouver indies (NecroDancer, Beastieball).

### Cited Findings
**Jamie Cheng**
- Founded Klei Entertainment in July 2005 in Vancouver, funded by selling his THQ shares and a $10,000 loan from his brother. Previously a specialist AI programmer at Relic Entertainment — [Wikipedia: Klei Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klei_Entertainment)
- Studied computer science at Simon Fraser University. An internship at Relic led to an AI programming job. Credited with AI on *Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War* and *The Outfit*. Spent nearly three years at Relic before founding Klei — via search summaries of [CDM workshop presenter page](https://thecdm.ca/people/workshop-presenters/jamie-cheng) and [Business in Vancouver](https://www.biv.com/news/jamie-cheng-8238368) (not opened in full; medium confidence)
- Listed as CEO (in a 2017 context) — [Wikipedia: Klei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klei_Entertainment)
- Interviews: [Game Developer Q&A on Shank](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/q-a-klei-founder-cheng-discusses-i-shank-i-animation-repression); [Road to the IGF: Eets](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/road-to-the-igf-klei-entertainment-s-i-eets-i-)

**Jeffrey Agala**
- Co-founder and Creative Director of Klei — [Wikipedia: Klei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klei_Entertainment)

**Nick Waanders (added)**
- Founder of Slick Entertainment, which Klei acquired in 2017. He had worked with Cheng at Relic — search summary of Klei history mirrors ([cavac.at mirror](https://cavac.at/cavacopedia/Klei%20Entertainment), low confidence; verify on Wikipedia)

**Klei, the studio**
- Games: *Eets* (2006), *Shank* (2010), *Shank 2* (2012), *Mark of the Ninja* (2012), *Don't Starve* (2013), *Don't Starve Together* (2016), *Invisible, Inc.* (2015), *Oxygen Not Included* (2019), *Hot Lava* (2019), *Griftlands* (2021), *Rotwood* (2026), *Don't Starve Elsewhere* (2026) — [Wikipedia: Klei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klei_Entertainment)
- Published *Crypt of the NecroDancer* (2015) and *Rift of the NecroDancer* (2025) for Brace Yourself Games, and *Beastieball* (early access 2024) for Wishes Unlimited — [Wikipedia: Klei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klei_Entertainment)
- Became a Tencent subsidiary on January 25, 2021 (majority stake). Klei says it keeps full creative control — [Wikipedia: Klei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klei_Entertainment)

### Inferences
- Klei bridges AAA and indie: a Relic alumnus founder, plus a publishing arm that backed at least two Vancouver indies (Brace Yourself, Wishes Unlimited). It suits a "mentor/elder" role in the narrative.

### Gaps
- Jamie Cheng: no birth year, hometown or pronouns found. 2026 status (still CEO?) not confirmed. No photo source found. Klei's site or press kit should be checked.
- Other early Klei leaders (e.g., producers or art leads on *Shank*/*Mark of the Ninja*) weren't researched.

## 3. Colin and Sarah Northway (Northway Games)

### Takeaway
A wife-and-husband team who began making games while at the University of Victoria. They spent years as digital nomads after *Fantastic Contraption* (2008) and settled in Vancouver in 2014. Sarah is the main active developer now (*Rebuild*, *I Was a Teenage Exocolonist*).

### Cited Findings
- Started making indie games in the 2000s while at the University of Victoria. Settled in Vancouver in 2014, where they have friends and family — [Georgia Straight 2024](https://www.straight.com/tech/how-vancouver-became-a-hub-for-indie-video-games)
- Sarah worked in game development in San Francisco before going indie. Colin previously made websites for lawyers. His 2008 Flash game *Fantastic Contraption* launched their indie careers — [Georgia Straight 2024](https://www.straight.com/tech/how-vancouver-became-a-hub-for-indie-video-games)
- Sarah on the early scene: "It was just this really small community in 2010." Colin: "Indie House is huge in the history." Colin also hopes Vancouver will host a game festival — [Georgia Straight 2024](https://www.straight.com/tech/how-vancouver-became-a-hub-for-indie-video-games)
- Games: *Rebuild* (2011), *Rebuild 2* (2011), *Incredipede* (2012; 2011 TGS Sense of Wonder Night; 2013 IGF Excellence in Visual Art nominee), *Deep Under the Sky* (2014), *Rebuild 3: Gangs of Deadsville* (2015), *Fantastic Contraption* VR (2016; IGF Nuovo nominee), *I Was a Teenage Exocolonist* (2022). Press coverage of their digital nomad lifestyle — [Wikipedia: Northway Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northway_Games)
- Roles: Colin makes physics puzzlers (*Fantastic Contraption*, *Incredipede* with art by Thomas Shahan, *Deep Under the Sky*). Sarah makes narrative strategy/sim games (*Rebuild* series, *Exocolonist*). *Exocolonist* was released August 25, 2022, published by Finji — [Northway Games press kit](https://northwaygames.com/presskit/) (via search summary)
- Studio based in Vancouver; press kit gives founding year 2011. The press kit calls Sarah the sole active member, while Colin's site says he's back at Northway Games and both are working on unannounced games — [Northway Games press kit](https://northwaygames.com/presskit/) (via search summary; the two statements may be from different dates)
- Interviews: [Game Maker's Notebook podcast](https://gamemakersnotebook.libsyn.com/sarah-and-colin-northway-discuss-i-was-a-teenage-exocolonist); [Game Developer on Exocolonist](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/-i-was-a-teenage-exocolonist-turns-life-experiences-into-a-card-game)
- Davey Wreden stayed with the Northways on a 2015 vacation before moving to Vancouver in 2019 — [Georgia Straight 2024](https://www.straight.com/tech/how-vancouver-became-a-hub-for-indie-video-games)
- Website: northwaygames.com — [press kit](https://northwaygames.com/presskit/)

### Inferences
- The Northways are a Victoria-to-Vancouver link and plausible witnesses to the Victoria meetup that seeded Full Indie (timeline gap). They also act as "hosts" who drew other developers (Wreden) to the city.

### Gaps
- No birth years, hometowns or pronoun statements found. Photo: the Northway press kit likely has team photos (not confirmed). Colin's personal website URL not captured.

## 4. Justin Smith (Enviro-Bear 2000, Desert Golfing, Captain Games)

### Takeaway
A notoriously low-profile Canadian solo developer. Enviro-Bear (2009) was a 2010 IGF Nuovo finalist, and Desert Golfing (2014) was released under Blinkbat Games. Later work is under Captain Games (*Golf on Mars*, *Horsey Game* in 2026). He's widely described as Vancouver-based, but there's little personal detail.

### Cited Findings
- Canadian independent developer; made *Enviro-Bear 2000* in about two months for TIGSource's Cockpit Competition, with MS Paint art, expecting a "complete failure." Inspired by a Box2D test app. Attended 2010 Fantastic Arcade (Austin), where it won an award — [Wikipedia: Enviro-Bear 2000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enviro-Bear_2000)
- *Desert Golfing* was developed and published by Blinkbat Games. Smith later released *Golf on Mars* as Captain Games — [Wikipedia: Desert Golfing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desert_Golfing)
- Described as "Vancouver-based" in a 2014 Medium profile (third-party blog; I couldn't open it, 403) — [Medium, Alec Thomson](https://medium.com/@alecthomson/the-end-of-desert-golfing-and-other-stories-from-mysterious-canadian-justin-smith-5179952261ac)
- Interviews: [7 Questions for Desert Golfing creator (Game Developer, 2014)](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/7-questions-for-i-desert-golfing-i-creator-justin-smith); [Bear Theft Auto (Game Developer, 2010)](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/interview-bear-theft-auto---the-indie-adventures-of-i-enviro-bear-i-s-justin-smith); [Road to the IGF](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/road-to-the-igf-justin-smith-s-i-enviro-bear-2000-i-)
- *Horsey Game* (Feb 2026, Captain Games) — [Giant Bomb](https://giantbomb.com/wiki/Games/Horsey_Game) (from timeline; not re-checked); [Giant Bomb: Captain Games](https://giantbomb.com/wiki/Companies/Captain_Games)

## 5. Full Indie founders and leaders: Jake Birkett, Alex Vostrov, Aryo Nazaradeh

### Takeaway
Full Indie was founded in May 2010 by Jake Birkett and Alex Vostrov. Birkett's studio, Grey Alien Games, is now firmly UK-based. Vostrov is a Vancouver solo developer (Rocket Bear Games). Aryo Nazaradeh became president after returning from Toronto in 2022. Leaders between 2013 and 2022 are undocumented in what I found.

### Cited Findings
**Full Indie (context)**
- Founded May 2010 as monthly meetups by Birkett and Vostrov. FullIndie.com created in 2011. UK branch FullIndie.uk (formerly "Best of British") in 2012. First Summit in 2013. Over 2,000 members in 2014. No presidents or leadership changes named on Wikipedia — [Wikipedia: Full Indie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_Indie)

**Jake Birkett**
- Co-founded Full Indie in 2010 with Vostrov — [Georgia Straight 2024](https://www.straight.com/tech/how-vancouver-became-a-hub-for-indie-video-games)
- Founded Grey Alien Games in 2004; co-owns it with his wife, game developer Helen Carmichael. The studio is in Bridport, Dorset, UK. Games include *Xmas Bonus* (2005), *Regency Solitaire* (2015), *Shadowhand* (2017), *Ancient Enemy* (2020, with Jim Rossignol, supported by Bithell Games), *T-Minus 30* (2021) — [Grey Alien Games (Wikipedia mirror, 2023)](https://kaweah.freedombox.rocks/kiwix/wikipedia_en_all_maxi_2023-10/Grey_Alien_Games); [Ancient Enemy press kit](https://www.indiedb.com/games/ancient-enemy/presskit)
- Website: greyaliengames.com — [mirror](https://kaweah.freedombox.rocks/kiwix/wikipedia_en_all_maxi_2023-10/Grey_Alien_Games)

**Alex Vostrov**
- Co-founded Full Indie in 2010 with Birkett — [Georgia Straight 2024](https://www.straight.com/tech/how-vancouver-became-a-hub-for-indie-video-games)
- One-man studio Rocket Bear Games (previously Indiebird), based in Vancouver. Games: *Infested Planet* (first commercial game), *Attack of the Paper Zombies*, *Pandora's Gearbox*, *Swarm*. Left his day job to go full-time indie — [Game Developer: A Rocket Bear Takes Off](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/interview-a-rocket-bear-takes-off---vostrov-on-going-full-time-indie)

**Aryo Nazaradeh**
- Full Indie president. Took over after returning to Vancouver from Toronto in 2022, after the group stopped during Covid. Runs meetups and is exploring a return of the Summit. Got involved through an interest in game audio and networking. "The people here are really, really passionate." (He spoke to the Straight from Colombia.) — [Georgia Straight 2024](https://www.straight.com/tech/how-vancouver-became-a-hub-for-indie-video-games)

### Inferences
- Birkett is British and Grey Alien Games is now in Dorset, and the 2012 UK spin-off FullIndie.uk fits a return to the UK. But I found no source that dates his time in Vancouver or his move back. Treat this as a hypothesis to confirm in interview.

### Gaps
- When Birkett lived in Vancouver and when he left: undocumented in what I found.
- Full Indie leadership 2011–2022 (summit organizers, board) not found. A web search for "Aryo Nazaradeh" returned nothing relevant, so Full Indie's own site, LinkedIn or the 2025 DigiBC/LaSalle pages should be checked.
- No birth years or photos found for any of the three.

## 6. The TowerFall / Celeste / Extremely OK Games circle

### Takeaway
Maddy Thorson moved to Vancouver after college in Alberta, lived at Indie House, and prototyped *TowerFall* at the June 2012 Full Indie jam. The core EXOK team was Thorson, Noel Berry, Amora Bettany, Pedro Medeiros, Heidy Motta and composer Lena Raine. EXOK cancelled *Earthblade* in January 2025 after Medeiros' departure. Noel Berry is now making *City of None* (2027) with his brother Liam.

### Cited Findings
**Maddy Thorson** (she/her; current name used per request)
- Full name Madeline Stephanie Thorson. She/her (per her Mastodon, cited by Wikipedia). Born March 18, 1988 (Wikipedia cites her own 2009 blog post "Birthday Randomness") — [Wikipedia: Maddy Thorson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maddy_Thorson)
- Attended Grande Prairie Regional College, Alberta. After graduating she moved into a Vancouver apartment with Chevy Ray Johnston (no date given). Birthplace not stated — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maddy_Thorson)
- Lived at Indie House; at one point four of its seven residents were working on *TowerFall*. *TowerFall* came out of the 48-hour June 2012 Vancouver Full Indie Game Jam, where she worked with Alec Holowka — [Wikipedia: TowerFall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TowerFall)
- Incorporated her company in November 2012 (later renamed Maddy Makes Games). On September 5, 2019, announced it would continue as Extremely OK Games (EXOK), alongside a move to shared Vancouver offices. The name came from a tweet by operations manager Heidy Motta — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maddy_Thorson)
- Games: *TowerFall* (2013; lead creator/designer/programmer) and *Celeste* (2018; lead creator). Mario romhacks incl. *Super Mario World Remix* (2015) and *Masterpiece* (2025). GDQ Kaizo relay level design (2022, 2024). Director of R&D at EXOK since Sept 2019 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maddy_Thorson)
- *TowerFall* grossed over $500,000 across platforms by April 2014 — [Wikipedia: TowerFall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TowerFall)
- Websites: maddymakesgames.com; exok.com — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maddy_Thorson)
- **Photo:** Commons file "Maddy_Thorson_at_ACMI,_Melbourne_(retouched)_(cropped).jpg". License not verified — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maddy_Thorson)
- Rolling Stone profile on her trans identity — [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/culture/rs-gaming/maddy-thorson-towerfall-celeste-trans-stories-1234985446/) (paywalled, HTTP 402; contents not verified)

**Earthblade cancellation (Jan 2025): conflicting accounts**
- Wikipedia (Thorson and Bettany pages) cites a dispute over *Celeste* IP rights that led to departures, plus development struggles — [Wikipedia: Maddy Thorson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maddy_Thorson); [Wikipedia: Amora Bettany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amora_Bettany)
- Engadget: co-founder Pedro Medeiros parted ways in November (2024). The rift "was not the reason" for cancelling. Thorson said work had "become so exhausting… it has been a struggle for a long time" and "Pedro and the *Neverway* team aren't the enemy." Engadget doesn't mention an IP dispute — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/gaming/extremely-ok-games-has-cancelled-its-follow-up-to-celeste-000352550.html)

**Noel Berry**
- Programmer on *Celeste* (lead programmer for the 2018 release) with Thorson. Founding EXOK staff alongside Amora Bettany, Pedro Medeiros, Heidy Motta and Lena Raine — [Game Developer: Celeste devs form Extremely OK Games](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/-i-celeste-i-developers-form-new-studio-extremely-ok-games); [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/113801/celeste-and-towerfall-devs-form-new-extremely-ok-games-studio)
- Announced *Skytorn* in December 2014. Made at the "Vancouver-based IndieHouse" alongside the developers of *TowerFall Ascension* and *Night in the Woods*. Described himself as an "independent game developer from Canada" — [PlayStation Blog, 2014](https://blog.playstation.com/archive/2014/12/06/procedurally-generated-adventure-skytorn-announced-ps4/)
- *Skytorn* was paused to make *Celeste* and later cancelled — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/au/celeste-devs-cancel-skytorn-their-2d-action-game)
- Leads EXOK with Thorson (per Engadget, 2025). Revealed *City of None* on April 30, 2026: a metroidvania co-developed with his brother **Liam Berry**, published by EXOK, coming to PC/Switch/Switch 2 in 2027 — [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/2026/04/30/city-of-none-is-a-metroidvania-from-a-celeste-co-creator-and-it-launches-next-year); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/gaming/extremely-ok-games-has-cancelled-its-follow-up-to-celeste-000352550.html)

**Amora Bettany**
- Brazilian artist; birth name Raquel. Born 1987 in São Paulo (Wikipedia cites her "About me" page at amora.ink). Pronouns not explicitly stated (Wikipedia uses she/her) — [Wikipedia: Amora Bettany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amora_Bettany)

- Credits: *Out There Somewhere* (2012) and *Deep Dungeons of Doom* (2013) (design, art, writing); *TowerFall* (character design, concept art, localization); *Celeste* (2018: writing, concept art, animation, promo art, localization); *Celeste 64: Fragments of the Mountain* (2024, co-creator); *Wanderstop* (2025, concept art) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amora_Bettany)
- Left EXOK around the *Celeste* IP dispute (per Wikipedia). Based in Vancouver. Website: amora.ink — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amora_Bettany)

**Pedro Medeiros ("saint11")** (Brazil-based per request; not confirmed by my sources)
- Brazilian pixel artist and game developer; co-founder of MiniBoss (2010) and EXOK. Known for *TowerFall* and *Celeste* pixel art. Pixelates Bettany's concept art. Partner of Amora Bettany — [Wikipedia: Amora Bettany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amora_Bettany); [Wikipedia: TowerFall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TowerFall); [MiniBoss Tumblr](https://www.tumblr.com/studiominiboss)

**Heidy Motta (added)**
- MiniBoss co-founder (2010) and EXOK operations manager. Named "Extremely OK Games" in a tweet — [Wikipedia: Amora Bettany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amora_Bettany); [Wikipedia: Maddy Thorson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maddy_Thorson)

**Lena Raine** (Vancouver tie unconfirmed)
- Also known as Lena Chappelle and Kuraine. Trans woman; Wikipedia uses she/her. Born February 29, 1984 (Wikipedia cites her own 2018 tweet), in Seattle — [Wikipedia: Lena Raine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lena_Raine)

Website lena.fyi. Photo: Commons "Lena_Raine.jpg" (2018 promo; license not verified) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lena_Raine)

## 7. Greg Lobanov (Wandersong, Chicory, Beastieball / Wishes Unlimited)

### Takeaway
Born and raised in Philadelphia, a Drexel graduate who moved to the Richmond Indie House in 2015 at 22. He leads Wishes Unlimited (*Wandersong* 2018, BAFTA-winning *Chicory* 2021, *Beastieball* early access 2024 published by Klei).

### Cited Findings
- Born and raised in Philadelphia; attended Drexel University's Westphal College. Moved to Vancouver in 2015. Studio Wishes Unlimited ("Greg Lobanov & friends"); earlier worked as Dumb and Fat Games. Website wishesgames.com. Wikipedia lists his birth year as missing — [Wikipedia: Greg Lobanov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greg_Lobanov)
- Games (designer/programmer): *Pollushot* (2011), *Phantasmaburbia* (2012), *Perfection* (2014), *Coin Crypt* (2014), *Super Goo Goo* (2015), *Wandersong* (2018), *Chicory: A Colorful Tale* (2021; BAFTA for family game), *Beastieball* (early access 2024) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greg_Lobanov)
- Former Indie House resident; moved from Philadelphia to the Vancouver suburbs in 2015: "Vancouver was where I had the largest community of friends and other game developers that I knew of." Runs a four-person studio and leads a developer volleyball team — [Georgia Straight 2024](https://www.straight.com/tech/how-vancouver-became-a-hub-for-indie-video-games)

## 8. Night in the Woods: Alec Holowka, Scott Benson, Bethany Hockenberry

### Takeaway
Benson and Hockenberry lived in Pennsylvania (Pittsburgh), not Vancouver. Holowka (Winnipeg-born) worked in Vancouver early on, co-made the *TowerFall* jam prototype in Vancouver in 2012, and the "Indie House / Night in the Woods" link points to him. But I found no source that directly states his Vancouver residence dates, and by the IGF period he was described as working remotely from Winnipeg. He died in August 2019, days after abuse allegations.

### Cited Findings
**Alec Holowka**
- Alexander John Holowka; born October 30, 1983, Winnipeg; died August 31, 2019, Winnipeg (suicide) — [Wikipedia: Alec Holowka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alec_Holowka)
- Co-founded Bit Blot (with Derek Yu), Infinite Ammo and Infinite Fall. *Aquaria* (2007; IGF Seumas McNally Grand Prize). Music for *Crayon Physics Deluxe* (2009) and *TowerFall Ascension* (2013). Designer/programmer/composer of *Night in the Woods* (2017; BAFTA Narrative, IGF Grand Prize), crowdfunded with Scott Benson in 2013 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alec_Holowka)
- Early career: worked on an Xbox 360 combat racer in Vancouver. Wikipedia cites a YouTube video titled "Indie House / Night In The Woods" (Oct 2013) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alec_Holowka)
- Worked with Thorson at the June 2012 Vancouver Full Indie jam where *TowerFall* began; left the project shortly after — [Wikipedia: TowerFall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TowerFall)
- 2014: *Skytorn* developed at Vancouver's IndieHouse "alongside the developers of TowerFall Ascension and Night in the Woods" — [PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/archive/2014/12/06/procedurally-generated-adventure-skytorn-announced-ps4/)
- IGF-era description: "Holowka works on the game's code and musical score remotely from Winnipeg" — [Road to the IGF: Night in the Woods](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/road-to-the-igf-infinite-fall-s-i-night-in-the-woods-i-) (via search summary)
- **2019 (neutral account):** In August 2019, developer Zoë Quinn publicly accused Holowka of physical and emotional abuse (they briefly lived together in Winnipeg in 2012). The next day the *Night in the Woods* team cut ties. Benson wrote "We take such allegations seriously as a team" and said corroborating evidence had been presented to them. Publisher Finji supported the decision and postponed a physical release. Holowka died four days after the allegations. His sister's statement said he had been "battling mood and personality disorders," "was a victim of abuse," had been seeking therapy and medication, and "said he wished the best for Zoë and everyone else" — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alec_Holowka)
- **Photo:** Commons file "Alec_Holowka_2019_IGF.jpg". License not verified — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alec_Holowka)

**Scott Benson**
- Animator, designer, artist, (lead) writer and partial composer on *Night in the Woods*. Co-created *Longest Night* (2013). In The Glory Society, which announced *Revenant Hill* (May 2023); the studio closed in November 2023 and the game was cancelled after Benson's severe heart-disease diagnosis — [Wikipedia: Scott Benson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scott_Benson)
- Benson and Hockenberry live in Pittsburgh and have lived in Western/Central Pennsylvania for decades — [IGF: Night in the Woods](https://igf.com/night-woods) (via search summary)

**Bethany Hockenberry**
- Co-writer of *Longest Night*; designer, writer and researcher on *Night in the Woods*; member of The Glory Society — [Wikipedia: Scott Benson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scott_Benson); [IGF](https://igf.com/night-woods)

### Inferences
- The *Night in the Woods* "Indie House" link is most likely Holowka, physically present in Vancouver around 2012–2014. That's inferred from the 2012 jam, the 2013 "Indie House / Night In The Woods" video and the 2014 PlayStation Blog line, but not stated outright. Benson and Hockenberry should be listed as non-resident collaborators.
- Handle the 2019 events with attributed, sourced statements only. Avoid characterizing guilt or cause.

### Gaps
- Holowka's exact Vancouver residence dates and whether he lived at Indie House: unconfirmed.
- Benson and Hockenberry birth years and pronoun statements not found or checked.
