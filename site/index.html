Vancouver Indie Games Story

How did Vancouver become one of the world's great indie game cities? This is the working timeline behind a series of interviews with the people who built the scene. It's assembled from published sources, and every entry shows how well it's documented. The interviews will fill in the gaps, correct the record and tell the parts nobody wrote down.
